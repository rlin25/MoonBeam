# Design review trigger

When the user's entire message is the single keyword `DESIGN` (all caps, standalone), start a design
review conversation:

1. Ask the user to rubber-duck their initial concept — have them explain the idea in their own words
   first, before any questioning starts.
2. Then walk through Socratic questioning, **one question at a time**, waiting for their answer before
   asking the next.
3. For each area of ambiguity found in the concept:
   - State the issue plainly.
   - Give your top two suggestions for resolving it.
   - State which of the two you prefer.
   - Give your rationale for that preference.
4. Keep all explanations in plain English — no jargon dumps.
5. Continue this one-question-at-a-time loop until every meaningful blind spot in the concept has been
   covered, then stop.
